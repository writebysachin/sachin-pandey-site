# SachinPandey.com.np — Product Requirements Document

## Goal
Turn the personal website into a focused trust + lead-generation system for B2B marketing consulting, while naturally introducing Yoga Write Code (YWC) as a product built from real client problems.

## Recommended Positioning
"I help B2B companies build marketing systems that turn their website, SEO, and content into a compounding growth channel."
*Supporting idea:* Not more marketing activity. A system that compounds.

## Primary Audience
- B2B founders
- Small B2B marketing teams
- Professional-service businesses with a B2B growth problem
- Companies already doing marketing but lacking a repeatable system
*(Note: Do not claim a precise employee range unless validated by customer evidence.)*

## Business Goals
**Primary:** Generate qualified consulting conversations.
**Secondary:** 
- Build an email list through useful free resources/tools.
- Build authority through practical insights.
- Generate qualified users/feedback for Yoga Write Code.
- Turn consulting work into case studies and product insights.
**Not a goal:** Maximize page views, social followers, or generic email subscribers.

## Lead-Generation Model
Useful content / free tool → Immediate useful result → Optional email capture for deeper result/resource → Nurture → Consulting conversation or relevant YWC product interest.
*(Rule: Do not force email before providing value unless the resource genuinely requires it.)*

## First Lead Magnet
Start with ONE strong lead magnet.
**Recommended:** B2B Website & SEO Growth Diagnostic.
- Visitor enters website URL.
- Receives a concise diagnostic covering: Website conversion clarity, SEO fundamentals, Content structure, Internal linking/content opportunities, Messaging gaps, Recommended next actions.
- Offer: "Get the full diagnostic by email."
*(Rule: Only build this if the analysis can produce trustworthy results. Alternatives: B2B SEO/content planning checklist, Content opportunity worksheet, Website conversion + SEO audit template. Do not launch a tool zoo.)*

## Homepage Flow
1. Clear positioning
2. Problem
3. What Sachin helps with
4. Evidence / selected work
5. Free useful resource
6. How the engagement works
7. Insights
8. FAQ
9. Primary CTA

## Primary CTA
- **Primary:** Get a marketing systems diagnosis
- **Secondary:** Get the free B2B growth diagnostic
*(Rule: Avoid multiple competing CTAs.)*

## YWC Relationship
Present YWC as: "A product I'm building from problems I've seen repeatedly in client work."
The personal website should sell Sachin's expertise and generate conversations. yogawritecode.com should sell the software.

## Success Metrics
Track:
- Visitor → lead conversion
- Lead → qualified conversation
- Qualified conversation → client
- Free-tool starts
- Email opt-ins
- Email → conversation
- YWC referrals/signups

## Definition of Done
- [ ] Visitor understands who Sachin helps within 5 seconds.
- [ ] Visitor understands the problem.
- [ ] One primary CTA is obvious.
- [ ] One useful lead magnet exists.
- [ ] Lead capture works on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Confirmation email works.
- [ ] Privacy/terms links work.
- [ ] Analytics tracks source and conversion.
- [ ] No fake case-study metrics or unsupported claims.
- [ ] AI/product claims are accurate and demonstrable.
